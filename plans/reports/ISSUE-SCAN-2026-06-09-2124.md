## Finding 1: Data-node health flaps Down while replicated last_seen is fresh (per-manager-local false-DOWN, Bug CJ class, on v0.1.5)

- Severity: **high**
- Area: node / raft (monitor)
- Evidence:
  - `/api/v1/cluster/status` sampled 6x over ~12s oscillated
    `data_nodes_healthy` = **2 → 3 → 3 → 0 → 2 → 3**, `status` flapping
    `degraded ↔ ok` (anchor_pending steady at 1).
  - `/api/v1/nodes` sampled 8x over ~24s, committed `health` field per data
    node vs `last_seen_ms`:
    - `dtfs-prod-data01` (cold, LELI, co-located with managers): **Healthy in
      all 8 samples** — never flaps.
    - `dtfs-dc1-data01` (warm, DC1): **Down at samples 1, 4, 7**,
      Healthy otherwise — while `last_seen_ms` advanced steadily +5000 ms
      per heartbeat the entire time (134147 → 139148 → 144147 → 149147 →
      154148 → 159147). i.e. heartbeats keep arriving; health flaps Down
      anyway.
    - `dtfs-dc2-data01` (hot, huenfelden): Down at samples 4, 7.
    - At sample 7, dc1-data01's `last_seen` was only ~1.5 s staler than the
      freshest node yet was marked Down.
  - **Cross-manager same-instant disagreement (decisive)** — `/api/v1/nodes`
    health for dc1-data01 queried on all 3 voters in the same ~1s window:
    - round 1: mgr01 = **Down**, mgr02 = **Healthy**, mgr03 = **Healthy**
    - round 2: all Healthy
    - round 3: all Down
    The managers do **not** agree on a single value at a given instant, so
    health here is computed/held **per-manager**, not read from a single
    replicated last_seen. That is exactly the Bug CJ failure mode
    (monitor judging liveness from local heartbeat receipt), which #29 was
    supposed to fix by reading replicated `last_seen_ms`.
- Expected: with #29 in the build, all managers derive data-node liveness
  from replicated `last_seen_ms` and agree; a node whose `last_seen` is
  fresh (heartbeats arriving every ~5 s) stays Healthy.
- Actual: the two **network-distant** data nodes (DC1, huenfelden)
  flap Down on a ~9 s period while `last_seen` is fresh and advancing; the
  LELI-local data node never flaps; managers disagree at the same instant.
- Hypothesis: #29 is insufficient or regressed in v0.1.5 — the API/monitor
  health for `role==Data` is still driven by a per-manager local
  heartbeat-receipt signal (or a liveness window too tight for a ~5 s
  heartbeat interval plus cross-Tailscale latency to the LELI managers).
  Distance correlation (only the two remote nodes flap) points at
  arrival-time jitter, not actual node death — both nodes are otherwise
  reporting fresh stats. Confirming the exact mechanism needs the leader's
  monitor log lines (`heartbeat overdue` / `missed heartbeats` vs a
  last_seen comparison) — SSH, see handoff.
- Suggested next step: triage to re-open the monitor liveness path for
  `role==Data` on v0.1.5 — verify whether the health surfaced by
  `/api/v1/nodes` reads replicated `last_seen_ms` or a local map, and
  whether the down-threshold is ≥ 2.5-3x the heartbeat interval. This is
  the root cause feeding Findings 2 and 3.

## Finding 2: cache.promote raft-write flood on the 2 GB leader (mgr03) — imminent wedge risk

- Severity: **high**
- Area: migration / memory / raft
- Evidence:
  - `/api/v1/audit?limit=200` on the leader = **200 of 200 rows
    `action=cache.promote`**, `actor=tiering`, `result=promote_threshold_met`,
    every one `dst=14822802902563755837 (dc2-data01) tier=Hot dc=huenfelden`,
    targeting Austin Powers `.mkv` chunks at successive `idx=`.
  - Raft `last_log_index` on the leader grew **393380 → 393445 in 10 s
    (~6.5 entries/s sustained)**; earlier cross-read 392791 → 393224.
  - Leader is **mgr03** (`current_leader=1028174117865612657`) — per the
    cluster memory this is the **smallest box (2 GB RAM, swap previously
    full)** that swap-wedged under the 2026-06-08 flood.
- Expected: cache promotes fire occasionally (per `promote_window_secs`,
  throttled by `cooldown_secs`), complete, and stop; audit shows a mix of
  actions; raft log grows slowly at idle.
- Actual: the tiering engine emits cache.promote continuously for the same
  file's chunks, flooding the replicated log on the 2 GB leader at ~6.5
  writes/s and never converging.
- Hypothesis: downstream of Finding 1. The promote **target** dc2-data01
  (and the **source** dc1-data01) flap Down, so promotes never complete and
  the hot cache replica never establishes; with the volume's
  `cooldown_secs:0` + `promote_reads:1` (Finding 4) every subsequent Plex
  read re-fires the promote immediately → unbounded re-queue. This is the
  same flap → raft-write-flood → 2 GB-leader-wedge chain as the 2026-06-08
  incident, with cache.promote as the flood actor instead of
  UpdateNodeStatus/replication.schedule.
- Suggested next step: triage to (a) treat as the recurrence it is — fixing
  Finding 1 should drain it; (b) consider an interim operator lever (move
  raft leadership off the 2 GB mgr03; dampen the promote loop via Finding 4)
  — operator/mutating, not for the finder. Quantify mgr03 RSS/swap headroom
  via SSH before it wedges (handoff).

## Finding 3: 344 migration tasks stuck pending, never draining

- Severity: **high**
- Area: migration / replication
- Evidence:
  - `/api/v1/migrations` `count=344`, **every task `status=pending`,
    `attempts=0`, `last_error=null`**.
  - Breakdown by tier: **282 hot**, **62 warm**. The 282 hot tasks are the
    cache promotes of Finding 2 (`src=dc1-data01 dst=dc2-data01`,
    `task_id` up to `repl-58232`). The 62 warm tasks are warm-mandatory
    backfill (`mandatory_replicas.warm=1`) to the only warm node,
    dc1-data01.
  - Each distinct `chunk_id` appears once (no single-chunk re-queue within
    the snapshot) but the queue does not drain across reads minutes apart,
    and `attempts` stays 0 — tasks are created but never attempted.
- Expected: tasks dispatch (attempts climb), copy+verify, reach Done, queue
  drains; warm-mandatory backfill progresses.
- Actual: 344 tasks sit at attempts:0/pending; both target classes point at
  the two flapping nodes (hot→dc2-data01, warm→dc1-data01).
- Hypothesis: downstream of Finding 1 — `validate_task_target` /
  scheduler dispatch fast-fails or skips tasks whose target node is
  (intermittently) Down, so they never leave Pending; the tiering engine
  meanwhile keeps creating more (Finding 2). Warm-mandatory backfill to
  dc1-data01 is collateral — it stalls whenever dc1 flaps Down.
- Suggested next step: triage to confirm the dispatch path drops/skips
  tasks whose target is flapping Down, and that the 282 hot promotes are
  being re-created rather than retried. Fixing Finding 1 unblocks both
  target classes.

## Finding 4: volume `default` promote policy amplifies the flap into a flood

- Severity: **medium**
- Area: volume / config
- Evidence: `/api/v1/volumes/default` `cache_replicas`:
  `promote_reads:1`, `promote_window_secs:86400`, **`cooldown_secs:0`**,
  `per_dc:true`, `hot:{min:1,max:1}`, warm/cold/tape min:max = 0.
- Expected (AGENTS.md § A.3.4): a cooldown throttles re-promotion after a
  demote/eviction so a single read does not immediately re-arm a promote;
  `should_clear_cooldown_on_demote` only clears it when in the past.
- Actual: `cooldown_secs:0` means there is nothing to throttle re-promotion,
  and `promote_reads:1` means a single Plex read re-arms a hot promote
  instantly. With the target flapping (Finding 1) so the replica never
  establishes, this turns a transient into a hot loop (Finding 2).
- Hypothesis: config drift / aggressive defaults — not the root cause, but
  the multiplier that makes the flap a 6.5 writes/s flood rather than a slow
  trickle. `per_dc:true` + `hot{min:1}` legitimately wants a hot replica in
  the reading DC (huenfelden), which is structurally fine once the node
  stops flapping.
- Suggested next step: triage to scope a `cooldown_secs` > 0 (and possibly
  `promote_reads` > 1) on volume `default` as an interim damper —
  operator/mutating action, performed by the implementation/triage session,
  not the finder.
